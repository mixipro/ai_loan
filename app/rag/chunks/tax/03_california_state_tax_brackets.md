# California State Income Tax — 2024 Brackets

## Progressive structure

California has the HIGHEST state income tax in the US, with 9 brackets:

### Single filers 2024

| Income range | Rate |
|--------------|------|
| $0 - $10,412 | 1.00% |
| $10,412 - $24,684 | 2.00% |
| $24,684 - $38,959 | 4.00% |
| $38,959 - $54,081 | 6.00% |
| $54,081 - $68,350 | 8.00% |
| $68,350 - $349,137 | 9.30% |
| $349,137 - $418,961 | 10.30% |
| $418,961 - $698,271 | 11.30% |
| $698,271+ | 12.30% |

### Married Filing Jointly 2024

Brackets are roughly doubled (e.g., 12.3% starts at $1,396,542).

## Mental Health Services Tax

Additional 1% on income over $1M (regardless of filing status).
Funds California Mental Health Services Act (Prop 63, 2004).

## Combined federal + state for high earners

Bay Area Software Engineer earning $250k:
- Federal: 24% marginal
- California: 9.3% marginal
- **Combined marginal: 33.3%**

Bay Area exec earning $1.5M:
- Federal: 37% marginal
- California: 12.3% + 1% mental health = 13.3% marginal  
- **Combined marginal: 50.3%**

## Tax optimization strategies

1. Maximize 401(k) ($23,000/year limit) — reduces both fed + state
2. HSA contributions if eligible ($4,150 individual)
3. Tax-loss harvesting on stock gains
4. California muni bonds (DOUBLE tax-free — federal + state)
5. Charitable remainder trusts for large liquidity events
