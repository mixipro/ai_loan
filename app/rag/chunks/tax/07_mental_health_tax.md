# California Mental Health Services Tax (MHST)

## What it is

Additional 1% surcharge on California taxable income over $1,000,000.
Passed via Proposition 63 in 2004, funds Mental Health Services Act.

## How it works

This is ON TOP of California's 13.3% top marginal rate.
So highest effective state rate for income over $1M is: **14.3%**

## Calculation example

Tech executive earning $2.5M from RSU vesting:

| Bracket | Income in bracket | Rate | Tax |
|---------|------------------|------|-----|
| Up to $698k | $698,271 | progressive avg ~9% | ~$63k |
| $698k - $2.5M | $1,801,729 | 12.3% | $221,613 |
| MHST (over $1M) | $1,500,000 | 1.0% | $15,000 |
| **Total CA tax** | | | **~$300,000** |

Plus federal taxes brings total to roughly $1.1M out of $2.5M (44%).

## Affects on financial planning

For high earners (tech executives, real estate moguls, entertainment stars):
- IPO/exit events often trigger MHST
- Smooth out income across multiple years if possible
- Charitable strategies become more impactful
- Tax residency planning (move to Nevada/Texas) becomes attractive

## Strategies to minimize

1. **Income smoothing**: Spread RSU vests across years
2. **Charitable donations**: Reduce taxable income via DAF (donor-advised fund)
3. **Retirement contributions**: Max all tax-advantaged accounts
4. **Opportunity Zones**: Defer + reduce gains
5. **Tax residency change**: Some millionaires move pre-liquidity event

## Important note

MHST does not apply to capital gains specifically — it applies to total 
California taxable income. So planning is about overall income management.
