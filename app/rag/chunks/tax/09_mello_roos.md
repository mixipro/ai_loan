# Mello-Roos Special Tax Assessments

## What it is

Mello-Roos Community Facilities District (CFD) Act of 1982 allows local 
governments to levy SPECIAL TAXES on property within designated districts 
to fund public services and infrastructure.

This is IN ADDITION to standard Prop 13 property tax.

## Why it exists

After Prop 13 (1978) capped traditional property taxes, local governments 
needed new funding mechanisms for new development infrastructure (schools, 
roads, fire stations, parks).

## How much it costs

Mello-Roos adds approximately 0.5% to 2% on top of standard 1% property tax.

Example: $600,000 home in Inland Empire Mello-Roos district:
- Standard Prop 13: $6,000/year
- Mello-Roos: $4,200/year (0.7% additional)
- **Total: $10,200/year** (1.7% effective)

vs. Same home in non-Mello-Roos area: $6,000/year (1.0% effective)

## Common Mello-Roos areas in California

- **Inland Empire**: Many newer subdivisions in Riverside/San Bernardino
- **Sacramento suburbs**: Roseville, Folsom newer developments
- **Orange County**: Newer developments in Irvine, Lake Forest
- **Eastern Bay Area**: Some Tri-Valley areas
- **San Diego**: 4S Ranch, Otay Ranch, Eastlake

## Duration

Mello-Roos taxes typically last 25-40 years (matching bond term).
After bonds are paid off, the special tax ends.

## How to check before buying

1. **Title report** lists CFD assessments
2. **County tax assessor website** shows current Mello-Roos
3. **Ask seller's disclosure** (required by law in California)
4. **Look at property tax history** (Mello-Roos boosts effective rate above 1.2%)

## Strategy implications

- Always factor Mello-Roos into total cost of ownership
- Newer developments more likely to have it
- Established neighborhoods (pre-1990s) usually don't
- Some buyers specifically avoid Mello-Roos areas
- Resale impact: properties with Mello-Roos may have lower resale value

## Common misconception

Mello-Roos is NOT a "Prop 13 loophole" — it's a separate special tax that 
voters in the district approved. You agreed to it (legally) when buying.
