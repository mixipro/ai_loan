# 1031 Exchange — Defer Capital Gains on Property

## What it is

Section 1031 of IRS code lets you swap one investment property for another 
and DEFER all capital gains taxes indefinitely.

## Rules

- Like-kind property (real estate for real estate)
- Same or higher value (no taking cash out without tax consequences)
- 45-day identification window (identify replacement property)
- 180-day close window (complete the exchange)
- Must use Qualified Intermediary (cannot touch funds yourself)

## California-specific twist

California requires "California Source Income" tracking. If you 1031 exchange 
a California property INTO an out-of-state property, you still owe California 
tax when you eventually sell the replacement property.

This means: you can't escape California tax just by moving the property.

## Strategy: 1031 + Step-up Basis

Powerful combined strategy:
1. Hold 1031'd property until death
2. Heirs inherit at "stepped-up basis" — all deferred gains WIPED
3. Combined with 1031, you can build real estate empire tax-free across generations

## Example

Investor buys SF apartment building for $1M in 2010.
By 2025, worth $3M (would owe $700k+ in taxes if sold).

Options:
- Sell now: $700k tax bill
- 1031 to Sacramento commercial property worth $3.2M: $0 tax bill
- Hold until death: heirs get $3.2M property tax-free (Prop 19 may apply for non-spouse heirs)

## Common mistakes

- Missing the 45-day identification window (no extensions)
- Touching the funds during exchange (disqualifies)
- Failing to use Qualified Intermediary
- Trying to 1031 personal residence (only investment property qualifies)
