# California LLC Franchise Tax

## The $800 minimum

California charges every LLC a MINIMUM franchise tax of $800 per year, 
regardless of revenue or profit. This applies even to:
- LLCs with $0 revenue
- LLCs in their first year of operation (after first year exemption)
- Single-member LLCs (disregarded entities)
- Foreign LLCs registered in California

## First-year exemption

LLCs formed in 2021 or later get first-year exemption (no $800 fee in year 1).
This was a temporary COVID-era provision.

## Tax on gross receipts (additional)

For LLCs with gross receipts over $250,000:

| Gross Receipts | Additional Fee |
|----------------|---------------|
| $250k - $499,999 | $900 |
| $500k - $999,999 | $2,500 |
| $1M - $4,999,999 | $6,000 |
| $5M+ | $11,790 |

So a $1.2M revenue LLC pays $800 + $6,000 = $6,800/year minimum.

## Alternative: S-Corporation

For high-revenue solo entrepreneurs, S-Corp may save money:
- S-Corp franchise tax: 1.5% of net income, minimum $800
- LLC tax: based on gross receipts (above)

Break-even point: roughly $80k-100k net income makes S-Corp cheaper.

## Strategy implications

- Don't form LLC unless you have real business activity (the $800 hurts)
- For high-revenue solo: consider S-Corp instead
- For real estate investment: LLC is often worth it for liability protection
- Multi-member LLCs and partnerships have additional considerations

## Compliance

- Form 568 (LLC Return of Income) — due March 15
- Estimated payment Form 3522 — due April 15 of taxable year
- Late penalty: $250 minimum + interest
