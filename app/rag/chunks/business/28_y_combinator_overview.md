# Y Combinator — Premier Startup Accelerator

## What YC is

Y Combinator (founded 2005) is the most prestigious startup accelerator in the world.
Located in Mountain View, California.

## YC by the numbers

- Founded: 2005 by Paul Graham
- Total startups accelerated: 4,000+ (as of 2024)
- Active alumni: 3,000+ companies
- Combined value: $600B+
- Notable alumni: Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, DoorDash, Coinbase, Reddit

## Standard YC deal (2024)

Y Combinator invests in startups via standard terms:
- **$500,000 total investment**
  - $125,000 for 7% equity (SAFE)
  - $375,000 on MFN SAFE (Most Favored Nation, no cap)
- **Batch program**: 3 months in San Francisco
- **Demo Day**: Pitch to investors after program
- **Lifetime network access**

## Application and acceptance

YC application process:
- Online application (~6 weeks before batch)
- Video interview if selected
- Acceptance rate: ~1-2%
- Two batches per year (Winter + Summer)
- 200-400 startups per batch

## What YC provides

Beyond the $500k:
- Office space in Mountain View
- Office hours with partners (Garry Tan, Geoff Ralston, etc.)
- Network of alumni (huge value)
- Demo Day audience of top VCs
- Bookface (alumni community)
- Standardized legal docs (SAFE)

## YC alumni outcomes

What happens after YC:
- ~70% raise additional venture capital
- ~10% become "unicorns" ($1B+ valuation)
- ~20% acqui-hires or shutdowns within 2 years
- Top alumni reach billion-dollar IPOs

## Most successful YC alumni

Billion-dollar+ YC companies:
- Stripe ($90B+ valuation)
- Airbnb ($90B+)
- Coinbase ($65B IPO)
- DoorDash ($55B+)
- Instacart ($10B+)
- Reddit ($10B+)
- Dropbox ($10B+)
- Twitch ($1B+ acquisition by Amazon)

## When YC makes sense

YES, apply if:
- First-time founder needing structure + network
- Tech/software-focused startup
- Already have working prototype
- Open to relocating to Bay Area for 3 months
- Want best-in-class VC network introductions

## When to skip YC

NO, consider alternative if:
- Already established company with $1M+ revenue
- Industries YC doesn't focus on (heavy regulation)
- Founder team can't commit 3 months in Bay Area
- Prefer to bootstrap without dilution

## Alternatives to YC

Other top-tier accelerators:
- **Techstars** (multiple cities, similar terms)
- **500 Global** (less selective, broader)
- **Plug and Play** (corporate-focused)
- **Industry-specific**: SOSV, BoxGroup, etc.

## Strategy implications

- YC is the gold standard for early-stage acceleration
- $500k + network access > pure $500k from any source
- Apply with strong team and clear progress
- Even rejected applicants benefit from interview prep
- Bay Area location for batch is required
- Consider YC for first venture, less for repeat founders
