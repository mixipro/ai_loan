# Sand Hill Road — Bay Area VC Landscape

## What Sand Hill Road is

A 4-mile stretch in Menlo Park, CA where dozens of top VC firms cluster.
Has become synonymous with "elite venture capital."

## Top-tier VCs and their fit

### For consumer/marketplace
- **Sequoia Capital**: Apple, Google, Airbnb, Stripe
- **Benchmark**: eBay, Uber, Twitter, Snapchat
- **Accel**: Facebook, Slack, Atlassian

### For deep tech/enterprise
- **Khosla Ventures**: Square, DoorDash, Affirm
- **Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)**: Facebook, Lyft, Slack
- **Greylock**: Airbnb, LinkedIn, Workday, Discord

### For B2B SaaS
- **Bessemer Venture Partners**: Shopify, Twilio, Pinterest
- **Insight Partners**: Twitter, Tumblr
- **Lightspeed**: Snapchat, Affirm, Carta

## Stage focus

VCs specialize by stage:

### Pre-seed/Seed
- Y Combinator (accelerator)
- Initialized Capital
- Founders Fund (Peter Thiel)
- First Round Capital
- Floodgate

### Series A
- Most top-tier firms have Series A funds
- $5-15M typical check
- Valuations $20-100M

### Growth stage
- General Atlantic
- Insight Partners  
- TCV
- IVP

## How VCs make money

Standard VC economics:
- **2% management fee** annually on fund
- **20% carried interest** on profits
- Fund lifetime: 10 years typically
- Need 3-5 outliers to make fund work
- Most companies fail or return modest

## VC vs other capital sources

| Source | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| VC | Big checks, networks | Heavy dilution, control loss |
| Angels | Faster, supportive | Smaller checks |
| Revenue-based | No dilution | Slower growth |
| Bank loan | No dilution | Hard for startups |
| Crowdfunding | Marketing benefit | Lots of work |
| Bootstrap | Full control | Slower scaling |

## Fundraising tips

Best practices for raising from Sand Hill Road:
1. **Warm intro essential** (cold emails rarely work)
2. **Pitch deck under 12 slides** (clean, focused)
3. **Show traction first** (revenue or growth metrics)
4. **Know your numbers cold** (LTV/CAC, churn, growth)
5. **Multiple firms simultaneously** (creates urgency)
6. **Negotiate from term sheet positions** (not just one)

## Red flags VCs watch for

What kills deals:
- Cofounder conflicts
- Single dependency (one customer 50%+)
- Sketchy cap table
- No clear path to $100M+ revenue
- High customer acquisition cost
- Unfocused product/market
- Founder ego or inflexibility

## Strategy implications

- Bay Area access matters for VC raises
- Network/intros critical for top firms
- Be ready for term sheet negotiation
- Understand VC math (3-5x return needed)
- Plan for dilution at every round
- Consider non-traditional funding alternatives
