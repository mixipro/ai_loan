# California Cannabis Industry Business

## Industry status

California legalized recreational cannabis in 2018 (Prop 64).
Largest legal cannabis market in the world.

Industry scale:
- $5-8B legal sales annually (declining from peak)
- 12,000+ licenses issued (active operations)
- 75,000+ direct jobs
- $1B+ tax revenue annually

## License types

California has 22 license categories:
- **Cultivator**: 14 sub-types (size, indoor/outdoor)
- **Manufacturer**: 6 sub-types
- **Distributor**: 2 types
- **Retailer**: Storefront, delivery, microbusiness
- **Testing laboratory**: Independent verification
- **Microbusiness**: Cultivation + distribution + sale

## Major business opportunities

### Retail/dispensaries
- 1,800+ retail licenses
- $1-3M typical startup cost
- 60-200 retail locations per metro area
- High operational costs

### Cultivation
- Large-scale outdoor: $5-50M investment
- Indoor: $10-100M investment
- Lower margins than peak
- Consolidation occurring

### Manufacturing (extraction/edibles)
- Concentrates, vapes, edibles
- Higher margins
- Brand-building important
- High capital requirements

### Delivery
- Faster growth than storefront
- Lower overhead
- Technology platforms competing
- Statewide delivery licenses available

## Massive tax burden

California cannabis taxation is BRUTAL:

### State taxes
- **15% excise tax** on retail sales
- **Cultivation tax**: $9.65/oz flower (lifted Jan 2023)
- **State sales tax**: 7.25%
- **State income tax**: Standard 8.84% (C-Corp) or 13.3% (individual)

### Local taxes
- **Cities/Counties**: 5-15% additional gross receipts tax
- Total effective tax: 30-60% of revenue
- Higher than illicit market competitors

## Federal Section 280E nightmare

### What 280E does
- Federal tax provision treating cannabis as Schedule I drug
- DISALLOWS most business deductions
- Only Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) deductible
- Effectively taxes gross profit, not net income

### Real impact
Cannabis dispensary example:
- Revenue: $5M
- COGS: $2.5M
- Operating expenses: $1.8M
- Pre-tax profit: $700k

Federal tax calculation:
- "Taxable income" = $5M - $2.5M = $2.5M
- Federal tax (21%): $525k
- Effective tax rate: 75% of actual profit
- ROI severely compromised

## California 280E response (positive)

California state DOES allow deductions:
- AB 39 (2019): California decouples from 280E
- California treats cannabis like other businesses
- State income tax much lower than federal effective rate
- Mixed bag for California cannabis

## Banking challenges

Federal banking restrictions:
- Most banks refuse cannabis customers
- Cash-only operations common
- Robbery risk elevated
- Cannabis-friendly banks: ~50 in California
- High banking fees

## Capital intensive

Starting cannabis business requires:
- **Retail license**: $50-500k purchase price
- **Build-out**: $500k-2M
- **Inventory**: $300k-1M
- **Security**: $200k+
- **Working capital**: 6-12 months
- **Total startup**: $1.5-5M typical

## Industry challenges

Current cannabis industry struggles:
- **Illicit market still 60%** of total cannabis sales
- **High tax burden** drives users to illicit
- **Price compression** ($120/oz to $40/oz wholesale)
- **Consolidation accelerating**
- **Multiple bankruptcies** in major operators

## Strategy implications

### For aspiring cannabis entrepreneurs
- VERY HIGH CAPITAL requirements
- 280E federal tax burden remains
- Banking complexities never end
- Best for experienced operators
- Consider ancillary services instead

### Ancillary opportunities (less risky)
- Cannabis software/POS
- Real estate (lease to operators)
- Security services
- Marketing/branding
- Consulting/compliance
- Equipment manufacturing

### For California investors
- Cannabis investments highly risky
- Many startup failures
- Public cannabis stocks (Canopy, Tilray) struggle
- Real estate plays safer than direct ops
