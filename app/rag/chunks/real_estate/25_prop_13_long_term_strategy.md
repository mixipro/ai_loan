# Long-Term Prop 13 Strategy for California Investors

## The compounding savings

Prop 13's 2% annual cap on assessed value increases creates COMPOUNDING tax savings over time:

Year 10: Saving ~$2,000-3,000/year vs new buyer
Year 20: Saving ~$5,000-7,000/year vs new buyer  
Year 30: Saving ~$10,000-15,000/year vs new buyer

Cumulative 30-year savings: $150,000-300,000+ for typical California home.

## What this means for investors

1. **Long-term holds maximize Prop 13 value**
2. **Cash flow improves over time** (rent goes up, property tax stays nearly fixed)
3. **Effective tax rate decreases** as market value grows but assessment doesn't
4. **Selling resets the clock** (new owner pays based on current market)

## Optimal Prop 13 strategy

### Buy strategy
- Purchase early in California real estate career
- Hold LONG-TERM (10+ years minimum)
- Buy in growing markets (assessment lag compounds)

### Don't strategy  
- Don't flip properties (Prop 13 benefit reset)
- Don't transfer to corporate entities (often triggers reassessment)
- Don't make major improvements without considering reassessment impact

### Inheritance strategy
- Plan around Prop 19 (post-2021 rules)
- Encourage heirs to occupy as primary residence
- Consider trusts and gifting strategies

## Real-world example

Investor buys $400k rental property in Sacramento in 2015.
By 2026 (10 years later):
- Market value: $750k (estimated)
- Assessed value (Prop 13): $400k × (1.02)^11 ≈ $497k
- Property tax: ~$5,500/year
- New buyer would pay: $8,250/year
- **Annual savings: $2,750**

By 2045 (30 years later):
- Market value: ~$1.7M (5% annual appreciation)
- Assessed value: $400k × (1.02)^30 ≈ $725k
- Property tax: ~$8,000/year
- New buyer would pay: ~$19,000/year
- **Annual savings: $11,000**

## Combined with 1031 Exchange

For SCALING investors:
- 1031 Exchange preserves Prop 13 timing in some cases
- Like-kind property in California maintains some basis
- Out-of-state 1031: California "claws back" deferred tax later
- Complex but powerful when planned correctly

## Strategy framework

For California real estate:
1. **Hold long-term** to maximize Prop 13
2. **Focus on appreciation markets** (savings compound more)
3. **Calculate Prop 13 advantage** as part of ROI
4. **Plan estate transfers** to preserve basis where possible
5. **Don't underestimate** Prop 13's role in California real estate returns
