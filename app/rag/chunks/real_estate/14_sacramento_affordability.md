# Sacramento Region — Affordable California

## Median home prices (2024)

- Davis: $850k (university premium)
- Folsom: $750k
- Roseville: $700k
- Sacramento (city): $520k
- Elk Grove: $620k
- Stockton (Central Valley adjacent): $420k

## Why Sacramento is attractive

1. **Affordability**: 1/3 the price of Bay Area
2. **Government stability**: State capital = recession-resistant jobs
3. **Bay Area refugees**: Remote workers fleeing high-cost coastal areas
4. **Tech migration**: Increasing tech presence (less competitive than SV)
5. **Family-friendly**: Better schools per dollar than expensive CA areas

## Rental yields

BETTER than Bay Area or LA:
- Sacramento city: 5-6% gross
- Suburbs (Roseville, Folsom): 4-5%
- Stockton: 6-7% (but higher risk)

## Risk factors

- Wildfire (foothill areas)
- Flood (riverbed proximity)
- Government employment concentration
- Less appreciation than coastal CA

## Strategy implications

- Cash flow strategy works here (unlike Bay Area)
- Long-term appreciation slower but stable
- Good for first-time California investors
- Property tax with Mello-Roos in newer subdivisions (Folsom, Roseville)
- BRRRR strategy viable in older Sacramento neighborhoods

## Comparison vs. Bay Area

Same $700k buys:
- Sacramento: 3,000sqft family home with yard
- Bay Area: 800sqft 1-bedroom condo
