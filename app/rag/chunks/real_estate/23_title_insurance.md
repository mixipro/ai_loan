# Title Insurance in California Real Estate

## What it is

Title insurance protects you from:
- Forged deeds in chain of title
- Unknown liens or encumbrances
- Boundary disputes from old surveys
- Heirs of previous owners claiming ownership
- Fraud or impersonation in past transfers
- Errors in public records

## Two types

### Lender's Title Insurance (REQUIRED if loan)
- Protects the lender
- Required by mortgage company
- Paid for by buyer typically
- Coverage decreases as loan paid down

### Owner's Title Insurance (RECOMMENDED)
- Protects YOU as owner
- Optional but strongly advised
- Coverage stays same as property value rises
- One-time premium at closing

## Cost

California title insurance:
- Lender's policy: ~0.5% of loan amount
- Owner's policy: ~0.3-0.5% of purchase price
- Combined: ~1% of property value typical

Example: $1M California home:
- Combined title insurance: ~$8,000-12,000

## Why California title insurance differs

California has unique title challenges:
1. **Older properties** (especially LA, SF) have complex chain of title
2. **Mexican land grants** still affect some areas
3. **Mineral rights** sometimes severed from surface rights
4. **Easements** for utilities, access can be hidden
5. **Mello-Roos assessments** must be disclosed

## What's NOT covered

Title insurance does NOT cover:
- Issues that arise AFTER closing
- Building code violations (different insurance)
- Property condition (home warranty)
- Environmental hazards
- Tenant disputes

## Strategy implications

- ALWAYS get owner's title insurance (one-time cost, lifetime protection)
- Compare quotes from multiple title companies (rates vary)
- Review preliminary title report carefully
- Investigate any easements or encumbrances disclosed
- Title insurance is one of few items where shopping around saves money

## California-specific company list

Major California title insurers:
- **First American Title** (largest)
- **Fidelity National Title**
- **Stewart Title**
- **Old Republic Title**
- **Chicago Title** (now part of FNF)

All regulated by California Department of Insurance.
