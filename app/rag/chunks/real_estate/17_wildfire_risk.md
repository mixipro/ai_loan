# California Wildfire Risk and Insurance Crisis

## The insurance crisis

Since 2020, MAJOR insurers have stopped writing new policies in California:
- **State Farm**: Withdrew from new policies (May 2023)
- **Allstate**: Paused new policies
- **Farmers**: Limiting new policies
- **AAA**: Restricting coverage

This makes buying in high-risk areas EXTREMELY difficult.

## Affected areas

HIGH wildfire risk regions:
- Sierra Nevada foothills
- Wine country (Sonoma, Napa)
- Malibu and Pacific Palisades
- Mountain communities (Big Bear, Idyllwild)
- East San Diego County
- Parts of Inland Empire

## Cal Fire Hazard Zones

California maps property into zones:
- **Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ)**: Highest risk
- **High**: Significant risk
- **Moderate**: Lower but present risk
- Check at: fire.ca.gov

## Implications for buyers

1. **Difficulty getting mortgage** (lenders require insurance)
2. **California FAIR Plan** (last resort insurance) is expensive
3. **Property values declining** in high-risk zones
4. **Difficult to sell** later when buyer can't get insurance

## Strategy implications

- **ALWAYS check insurance availability BEFORE making offer**
- Budget extra $5,000-15,000/year for high-risk property insurance
- Consider defensible space requirements (vegetation clearance)
- Hardened construction (Class A roof, ember-resistant vents)
- Some buyers actively avoiding all wildfire zones now

## California FAIR Plan

State-mandated insurer of last resort:
- Available when no private insurer will write
- COVERS ONLY fire damage
- Need separate "wraparound" policy for theft, liability
- Expensive: often $3,000-10,000+ for FAIR Plan + wraparound
- Coverage limit only $3M

## Long-term outlook

Climate change increasing California wildfire risk.
- Insurance availability likely to continue tightening
- Property values in high-risk zones may continue declining
- Government interventions possible (more FAIR Plan capacity)
- Consider this in long-term ownership planning
