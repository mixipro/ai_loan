# Central Valley — Hidden California Real Estate Value

## Median home prices (2024)

- Stockton: $420k
- Modesto: $440k
- Fresno: $390k
- Bakersfield: $350k
- Visalia: $370k
- Merced: $380k

## Why Central Valley is overlooked

Most California investors focus on coastal markets, missing:
1. **Affordability**: 1/4 to 1/3 Bay Area prices
2. **High rental yields**: 6-8% gross common
3. **Population growth**: Bay Area migration accelerating
4. **Agricultural economic base**: Stable, recession-resistant
5. **Lower competition**: Less investor saturation

## Rental yield profile

EXCELLENT for cash flow investors:
- Single-family rentals: 6-8% gross yield
- Small multifamily: 8-10% gross yield
- After expenses: 4-6% net yield (cash flowing!)

## Industries

Central Valley economy:
1. **Agriculture** (largest sector) — almonds, dairy, wine, citrus
2. **Logistics** (Amazon, FedEx warehouses near highways)
3. **Healthcare** (regional medical centers)
4. **Education** (state universities)
5. **Manufacturing** (food processing especially)

## Risk factors

- **Drought** — agricultural impact major
- **Heat** — extreme summers (110°F+)
- **Air quality** — Central Valley has worst US air quality
- **Lower appreciation** than coastal CA
- **Limited tech industry** (vulnerability to manufacturing decline)

## Strategy implications

- **Cash flow strategies** dominate here
- **BRRRR** (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) viable
- **Multi-unit rentals** for serious investors
- **NOT for appreciation** (slower than coastal)
- **Distance management** if living elsewhere
- **Consider hot summer cooling costs** in budgeting
