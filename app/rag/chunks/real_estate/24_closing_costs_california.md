# California Real Estate Closing Costs

## Total closing cost estimate

California buyers typically pay 2-5% of purchase price in closing costs:
- $500k home: $10,000-25,000
- $1M home: $20,000-50,000
- $2M home: $40,000-100,000

## Major closing cost components

### Loan-related (if financing)
- **Loan origination fee**: 0.5-1% of loan
- **Appraisal**: $500-1,000
- **Credit report**: $30-100
- **Underwriting fee**: $400-900
- **Discount points (optional)**: 1% per point

### Title and escrow
- **Title insurance** (lender's + owner's): 1% of purchase
- **Escrow fee**: $2,000-5,000 (split with seller)
- **Notary fees**: $100-300
- **Recording fee**: $100-200

### Government/taxes
- **Property transfer tax**: $1.10 per $1,000 in most CA cities
- **Documentary transfer tax**: City-specific (SF: $5-30/$1,000)
- **Prepaid property tax**: Pro-rata for current year
- **Prepaid HOA dues**: If applicable

### Insurance and reserves
- **Homeowners insurance**: 1 year upfront (~$1,000-2,000)
- **Earthquake insurance**: If purchased
- **Reserves to lender**: 2-12 months property tax + insurance

## City transfer taxes (notable)

California has varying transfer taxes:
- **San Francisco**: $7.50-30 per $1,000 (graduated by price)
- **Los Angeles** (over $5M): Mansion Tax 4-5.5%
- **Oakland**: $11.50 per $1,000 (highest in Bay Area suburbs)
- **Berkeley**: $15 per $1,000
- **Most other cities**: $1.10 per $1,000

## Seller-paid closing costs

Sellers typically pay:
- **Real estate commission**: 5-6% of sale (split with buyer's agent)
- **Property tax due**: For period of ownership
- **HOA fees due**: For period of ownership
- **Some title fees**: Negotiable but often shared
- **Wood-destroying organism (termite) inspection**: $300-800
- **Home warranty (optional)**: $300-700

## Strategy implications

- Budget 3-5% extra for closing costs (don't undercapitalize)
- Negotiate shared costs (escrow, title)
- Get loan estimate within 3 days of application (TRID)
- Shop multiple lenders and title companies
- California Mansion Tax (LA) hits $5M+ properties — plan timing
- Closing cost credits from seller can be negotiated
