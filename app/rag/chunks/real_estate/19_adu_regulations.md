# Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) — California's Density Solution

## What is an ADU

An Accessory Dwelling Unit is a SECONDARY housing unit on a single-family lot:
- Detached (separate structure)
- Attached to main home
- Junior ADU (converted from existing space, max 500sqft)
- Garage conversion

## California laws making ADUs easier

Major laws enabling ADU construction:
- **AB 68 (2019)**: Streamlined ADU permits
- **AB 881 (2019)**: Eliminated owner-occupancy requirement
- **SB 9 (2021)**: Allows lot splitting for two homes
- **AB 2533 (2024)**: Further reductions in fees

## Cost to build

Typical California ADU costs:
- Garage conversion: $50k-150k
- Junior ADU (within existing structure): $30k-100k
- Attached new construction: $150k-300k
- Detached new construction: $200k-450k

## Income potential

ADU rental income (2024 baseline):
- Bay Area: $2,500-4,000/month
- LA: $1,800-3,000/month
- Sacramento: $1,200-1,800/month
- San Diego: $1,800-2,800/month
- Central Valley: $800-1,300/month

## ROI calculation

Example: SF Bay Area ADU
- Build cost: $250,000
- Monthly rent: $3,000 (gross $36k/year)
- Net (after expenses): $25,000/year
- Cash-on-cash return: 10% annually
- Plus property value increase: $200k-400k often

## Permitting

- Most California cities now have ADU-specific permits
- Process: typically 60-180 days
- Pre-approved plans available in some cities (faster)
- Fees varying $5k-25k depending on city

## Strategy implications

- ADU often the BEST single ROI improvement for California homeowner
- Effectively creates a duplex from single-family
- Tax: rental income, but expenses deductible
- Adds significant resale value
- Bay Area and LA particularly favorable
- Consider if you have garage to convert (lowest cost)
