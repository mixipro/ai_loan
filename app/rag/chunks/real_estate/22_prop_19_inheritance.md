# Proposition 19 — California Property Tax for Heirs (2020)

## What changed

Before Prop 19 (passed Nov 2020):
- Parent-child transfer: NO reassessment of property tax
- Children could inherit at parent's low Prop 13 basis

After Prop 19 (effective Feb 16, 2021):
- Parent-child transfer reassessed at MARKET VALUE
- UNLESS child uses as primary residence
- Even then, $1M exemption applies above original basis

## Practical example

Parent bought home in 1990 for $300k. Now worth $1.4M.

OLD law (pre-Prop 19):
- Child inherits at $300k basis
- Annual property tax: ~$3,200 (Prop 13 basis)
- Tax savings vs new buyer: $11,000+/year forever

NEW law (Prop 19):
- IF child moves in as primary residence: 
  - Up to $1M exemption above original basis
  - Effective basis: $300k + ($1.4M - $300k - $1M) = $400k
  - Annual tax: ~$4,200
- IF child uses as RENTAL or doesn't move in:
  - Full reassessment to $1.4M
  - Annual tax: ~$14,500

## Strategy implications

For California families:
1. **Encourage children to live in inherited property** for tax savings
2. **Investment property inheritance now much more expensive**
3. **Plan estate transfers BEFORE Prop 19 thresholds matter**
4. **Consider trusts and gifts during life** for some assets
5. **Senior portability**: Seniors can transfer base year value up to 3 times (improvement)

## Senior provisions (positive part of Prop 19)

For homeowners over 55:
- Can transfer property tax basis to new home 3 TIMES (anywhere in CA)
- New home can be MORE expensive (basis carries forward proportionally)
- Encourages older Californians to move (free up family homes)

## Disabled and disaster relief

Similar provisions for:
- Severely disabled homeowners
- Victims of declared disasters

## Impact on California real estate

- Investment property inheritance more expensive (encourages selling)
- Families with multiple homes face larger tax bills
- Some predict more property turnover (more homes coming to market)
- Multi-generational wealth transfer harder than before
- Increased state revenue from formerly-shielded transfers
