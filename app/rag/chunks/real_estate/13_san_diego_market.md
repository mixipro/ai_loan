# San Diego Real Estate Market

## Median home prices (2024)

- La Jolla: $2.5M
- Del Mar: $3M
- Coronado: $2.2M
- Carlsbad: $1.3M
- Encinitas: $1.5M
- San Diego (city): $850k
- Chula Vista: $700k

## Market drivers

San Diego economy unique:
1. **Biotech cluster** — Pfizer, Illumina, Qualcomm
2. **Military presence** — Navy creates stable demand
3. **Tourism** — beach communities, year-round climate
4. **Tech presence** — growing but not Silicon Valley scale
5. **University** — UCSD drives rental demand

## Rental yield profile

San Diego = balance of appreciation + cash flow:
- Coastal premium: 3-4% gross yield
- Inner suburbs: 4-5% gross yield
- University area: 5-6% (turnover-heavy)

## Earthquake/wildfire risk

- Earthquake: Moderate (active but smaller faults)
- Wildfire: HIGH in east county foothills
- Coastal flooding: Low-moderate in low-lying areas

## Unique factors

- Coastal Commission regulation (very strict)
- Military housing demand stabilizes market
- Cross-border (Tijuana) labor force impact
- Tech salaries lower than Bay Area (less buying power)

## Strategy implications

- Coastal = long-term appreciation, lower yield
- Inland = cash flow opportunities, higher yield
- Military areas = stable but limited upside
- University area = consistent rental demand
- Avoid east county wildfire zones unless special insurance secured
