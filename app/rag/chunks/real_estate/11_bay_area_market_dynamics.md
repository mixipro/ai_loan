# Bay Area Real Estate Market Dynamics

## Median home prices (2024 baseline)

- San Francisco: $1.4M
- Palo Alto: $3.2M
- Mountain View: $2.2M
- Oakland: $900k
- Berkeley: $1.5M
- San Jose: $1.3M
- Fremont: $1.5M

## Rental yield math

Bay Area rental yields are notoriously LOW. Typical:
- $1.4M SF home → $4,500/mo rent = $54k/year
- Gross yield: 3.85%
- After expenses (insurance, maintenance, vacancy, mgmt): 2.5-3%
- After mortgage costs: often NEGATIVE cash flow

## Appreciation play

Bay Area buyers don't profit from rent — they profit from appreciation.
Historical 30-year average: 7% annual.

## Risk factors

- Earthquake (San Andreas Fault — high probability over 30 years)
- Tech industry concentration (single industry downturn = market drop)
- Out-migration trend post-COVID (some areas declining)
- Housing supply constraints (limited new construction)

## Strategy implications

- Long-term holds (5+ years) historically profitable
- Cash flow strategies harder than appreciation strategies
- Prop 13 lock-in becomes major advantage over time
- Consider East Bay (Oakland, Berkeley) for better yields
- Tech industry exposure is a feature AND a bug
