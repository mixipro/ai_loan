# Los Angeles Real Estate Market

## Median home prices (2024)

- Beverly Hills: $4.5M
- Santa Monica: $2.2M
- Manhattan Beach: $2.8M
- West Hollywood: $1.6M
- Pasadena: $1.4M
- Long Beach: $850k
- Hollywood: $1.1M
- Inglewood: $750k

## Market characteristics

LA is more diverse than Bay Area:
- Beach communities: premium prices, lifestyle buyers
- Hollywood/Entertainment: industry-driven demand
- Suburbs (Pasadena, Glendale): family-oriented
- South LA (Inglewood, Compton): gentrifying, value plays

## Rental yields

Better than Bay Area generally:
- Beach communities: 3-4% gross
- Inner LA: 4-5% gross
- Inglewood/South LA: 5-6% gross (higher risk)

## Industry concentration

LA economy depends on:
1. Entertainment (Hollywood) — volatile
2. Tourism — recession-sensitive
3. Aerospace (LAX corridor) — stable government contracts
4. Trade (Port of LA/Long Beach) — global cycle

## Key risk factors

- Earthquake (multiple active faults)
- Wildfire (especially Malibu, Pasadena foothills)
- Insurance crisis (major insurers withdrawing from high-risk areas)
- Cost-of-living vs salary (lower than Bay Area incomes)

## Strategy implications

- Beach communities = appreciation play
- South LA = cash flow + future gentrification
- Avoid hilly fire-prone areas without insurance commitment
- Short-term rentals (Airbnb) often regulated heavily
